Messi vs Ronaldo GOAT Scoreboard

1. Keep index.html and goat-bg.png in the same folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome.
3. After an attempt, tap the matching GOAL or NO GOAL button.
4. The score updates on the picture and below it; voice chants use your browser/device voice (not real Messi/Ronaldo recordings).
5. Tap RESET SCORE to start over.

For best results, use Chrome with sound enabled. Internet is not needed for the background image; speech voice availability depends on the device.
